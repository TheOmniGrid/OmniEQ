## How to deliver the download on Ko-fi

Ko-fi has two ways; pick one and stay with it:

**A) Ko-fi Shop item (recommended for donationware)**
- Create a **digital shop item** "OmniEQ 1.0.0 (installer + portable)".
- Set the price to your minimum, or use *pay what you want* with a minimum.
- Attach both files (`OmniEQSetup.exe`, `OmniEQ-1.0.0-portable-win64.zip`). Ko-fi delivers them automatically after payment and keeps them downloadable for the buyer.
- Item description: use the *Welcome post* text from `PATREON.md`, minus the "attach" notes.

**B) Membership tier + members-only post**
- Same as Patreon: one tier, one pinned post with the files attached.

Either way, updates = upload the new files to the same item/post and mention the version.
